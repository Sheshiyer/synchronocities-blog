**Date:** 2026-07-18

## Rubedo correction: the legacy clean control did not survive v2.3

The original calibration selected `200-ok-mental-status-codes.md` because its v2.2 Nigredo audit reported `CLEAN`. Full v2.3 recertification invalidated that label: six rows now fail, including an undeclared HTTP/psyche synthesis, a Frankl misattribution, an over-universal mythic claim, a cross-tradition mode conflation, and two unsupported clinical generalizations. Independent clean-control review then rejected `the-fools-satchel.md` and five additional zero-failure ledger candidates because their ledgers omitted material source claims or assigned unsupported provenance locks.

Rubedo therefore retains the original file as an **invalidated legacy-clean/no-edit control**, not as evidence of cleanliness. Its required result is `PARTIAL`, byte-identical, with every v2.3 failing row enumerated. This preserves the calibration history while refusing to launder sparse claim extraction into a clean certification. No genuinely clean no-op control is claimed in this run; a fresh source-to-claim coverage pass is required before one can be selected.

## Selection Principle

The set samples one post for each requested Albedo grammar boundary. It deliberately does not take the eight highest-severity posts. Most cases are bounded local corrections; the one severe post is a no-edit negative control whose thesis-wide failures prove that manual routing is based on repair scope rather than a verdict count. This keeps the editable pilot safe while retaining science, mathematics, house-tradition, historical-attribution, causal-arrow, and escalation diversity.

All anchors were checked in both the Nigredo audit and the matching source post. SHA-256 values in the manifest freeze the read-only source inputs used for calibration.

## Why These Cases Are Not Interchangeable

1. The invalidated legacy-clean control tests whether Rubedo preserves evidence and refuses false certification.
2. The deletion-only case tests removal of invented precision where no replacement fact is needed.
3. The causal case tests arrow direction: chemistry may sign an experience but must not author it.
4. The quantitative case has a unique, tradition-defined replacement value and therefore forbids vague softening.
5. The synthesis case preserves a model but changes its epistemic label from verified fact to proposed correspondence.
6. The attribution case preserves a true 210-day calendrical structure while replacing a nonexistent named ceremony.
7. The decorative-math case removes prestige vocabulary because no mathematical structure is operated.
8. The escalation case forbids local repair when failure is distributed through the thesis and demands reconstruction.

Together, the set separates four operations that weak calibrations often collapse into "rewrite cautiously": delete, recode, correct, and relabel. It also brackets them with two no-edit outcomes: clean abstention and mandatory human escalation.

No case promotes `DECORATIVE` mathematics to `INTEGRATED` or upgrades a finding to load-bearing. The derived-synthesis case deliberately relabels the mapping as a proposal because correctness, demonstrated consequence, and independent provenance are not established; the decorative-math case deletes its unsupported label. The manual control is routed by thesis-wide repair scope, not by a legacy verdict count.

## Read-Only Handling

The files under `src/content/posts/` are evidence inputs only and remain read-only for this calibration-design task. The `expected_operation` and `hard_acceptance` fields describe how an Albedo implementation should behave on an isolated candidate copy. They are not instructions to mutate the canonical source posts during calibration-set construction.
