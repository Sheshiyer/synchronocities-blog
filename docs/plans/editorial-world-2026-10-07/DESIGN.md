# FIELDWORK design contract

Designing a private spatial planning game for Shesh Iyer on desktop and mobile web. The owner explores both brands, inspects source-backed work and chooses a proposed next day. The world must remain legible under real inventory density and preserve each public item's human approval gate.

## Reference lock

Primary: the supplied Cambium15-second company landscape and its deterministic Three.js source. Preserve an inhabited landscape with differentiated organ/place silhouettes, landscape-to-inspection continuity and source-grounded work. Temperance contributes brass/jade material contrast and the inspectable work sequence. The existing local Satoshi and Panchang files are the type source; no generic serif or imported font service.

Bounded secondary references: [Dorfromantik's official presskit](https://www.toukana.com/dorfromantik/presskit) describes a handpainted boardgame feel and quiet landscape planning; use territorial readability and scale. [Townscaper's publisher page](https://rawfury.com/games/townscaper/) describes coherent houses, arches, stairs and bridges; use differentiated architectural silhouettes and bridges. These references inform craft only. No game assets, interface or mechanics are copied. Publisher descriptions were read; no claim that their screenshot packs were imported.

Refero MCP is unavailable in this session. Its bundled typography, motion and anti-averaging references plus the supplied films form the research basis. Refero remains methodology; taste-skill supplies engineering checks, and stitch-design-taste supplies this enforceable contract. The non-SaaS/game brief supersedes default dashboard/Bento, framework-switch and perpetual-animation prescriptions. Reduced motion and purposeful motion take precedence over decorative loops.

## Visual theme

One direction: Cambium's inhabited company landscape rendered as an inspectable miniature expedition map. Density4/10; asymmetry7/10; motion3/10. A workshop, observatory, manuscript garden and publishing shore should differ by structure, not interchangeable spheres. A restrained field journal is an opened object, not a permanent dashboard sidebar.

## Palette and roles

- Field Charcoal `#111d22`: 3D canvas and low-emphasis navigation.
- Limestone `#e6e2d3`: opened journal paper and primary world text.
- Brass `#c5a46c`: sole interactive accent for focus, selection and intended work.
- Fog `#9caaa2`: contextual copy; contrast checked in the final visible state.
- Wood, mineral, vegetation and jade materials: world geometry/brand territory cues only. They do not become competing UI button accents.
- No indigo/purple, neon glow, generic gradient hero or pure-black material.

## Typography and spacing

Local Panchang display + Satoshi body/controls; bounded modular scale1.25, base16px. Small metadata may be12px on desktop, but must not carry the only way to perform an action. Body14–16px minimum, line-height1.5–1.7, max65ch. Title size uses clamp and never overlaps labels or the map. Space uses4/8px increments. Group captions use measured hierarchy instead of tiny serial-number noise.

## Composition and graph

Reserve clean screen zones for title/navigation, 3D map, opened journal and seven-day trail. No offscreen region label pretending its content is visible. Progressively disclose object labels rather than show every caption on top of every island. Hover/focus/selection has one clear beacon. Connections view retains landmarks and reveals edges. Edge meaning must stay explicit: editorial affiliation, exact source identifiers and draft destinations. No relationship is inferred from camera proximity or matching names.

## Interaction and states

Select landmark → inspect journal → choose proposed day → place/move on trail → reopen/export proposal. None of those actions grants publication authority. The journal must show exact draft body/title, identity, audience, destination, media and review hold. Native video controls; never autoplay audio. Changing plan date is visibly a proposal.

Find offers all487 observed records:12products,7drafts,330repositories,63hosts,22boards,53ideas. Search, category choices and record selection are keyboard operable. Record labels never show undefined. Empty trail communicates available days; storage/data/WebGL errors have actionable inline text. Focus returns to a usable control on journal close.

Desktop and mobile retain44px touch paths through Find and planning controls. Small-screen day navigation must remain usable without page overflow. Do not solve density by hiding source holds or exact copy.

## Motion and performance

Camera input and selection establish continuity; no forced autoplay camera orbit. Ambient movement is optional. Pause and prefers-reduced-motion suppress nonessential motion. Pause when hidden. Cap DPR; no new large dependency. Project labels via transforms rather than animate layout positions. Preserve keyboard/text access if WebGL fails. A final screenshot must be reviewed for geometry readability and label collisions; browser tests must show selection and proposal persistence.

## Quality gate

Verify reference lock, type/space/color tokens, clean region framing, readable paths, no clipped/overlapping labels, accessible selection, exact-copy/media journal, local proposal persistence, reduced-motion behavior and zero external submission effects. A successful syntax check is insufficient. Unverified mobile/performance limitations must remain stated in the verification receipt.
