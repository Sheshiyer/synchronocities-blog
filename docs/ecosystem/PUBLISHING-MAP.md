## Field Report route

[Owner-private Field Report](https://field-report.witnessalchemist.chatgpt.site) accepts dynamic candidates into persisted storage without rebuilding its bundled catalogue. [Implementation contract](../../.sites/field-report/docs/DYNAMIC-EDITORIAL-DRAFTS.md) covers the storage and revision behavior.

1. Store the candidate with current source/context, media and writer-policy bindings.
2. Review the complete public copy for meaning, attribution, assumptions and metaphors. Questions can be asked while a candidate is held.
3. Resolve the exact candidate's human questions. Editing copy, assets or bindings invalidates stale review; an older approval cannot transfer automatically.
4. The owner approves the exact current item and separately queues its publication handoff.
5. Delivery requires verified channel capability, account, destination, audience and email/media settings. Confirm the actual public platform readback and record its receipt.

Service authentication supports storage and inspection. It does not confer human approval identity. The existing [delivery contract](../editorial/nine-muses-2026-10-08/DELIVERY-CONTRACT.md) and source receipt boundaries remain in force. A generic Substack/X/Reddit destination mapping does not establish a working unattended adapter for every channel.
