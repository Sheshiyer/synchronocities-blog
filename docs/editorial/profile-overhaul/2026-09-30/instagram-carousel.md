## Slide copy

1. **A record before an explanation.**
Five fields for one ordinary event.
2. **Trigger**
What happened? Keep the description particular.
3. **Signal**
What did you notice, if anything?
4. **Felt shift**
What seemed different? Leave room for uncertainty.
5. **Action**
What did you do? Put consequences in a separate note.
6. **Story**
What did you think it meant? Keep an uncertain intention here as an interpretation.
7. **Read it again.**
Which part did you observe? Which part did you interpret?

## Caption

Try recording one ordinary event in five fields: trigger, signal, felt shift, action and story. Keep the story separate from what happened. Leave a field empty when you do not know.
