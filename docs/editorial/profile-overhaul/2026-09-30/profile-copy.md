## Personal website About

I’m Shesh Iyer, a builder and writer working at the meeting point of software, symbolic systems and everyday practice.

I’m building Tryambakam Noesis: tools and writing for examining how we notice, interpret and choose. The work takes several forms—software, essays, narrative and small practices that can be tried in an ordinary day.

The Why Chromosome is where I develop the questions at greater length. Synchronocities holds an essay archive and a tarot travelogue told through an interactive gallery. Across these forms, I’m interested in what a useful framework lets someone see—and what it might obscure.

You’ll find work in progress here: a design decision, a passage under revision, a tool being tested, or an observation that deserves a slower look. AI assists the making. I remain responsible for the claims, the choices and what gets published.
