## Standing rules applied throughout

**Mahābhārata story, verified assignment:** In [Ganguli's Ādi Parva I](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/m01/m01002.htm), **Ganesha requires that his pen never stop; Vyasa requires understanding before writing**. Vyasa then composes difficult verses that give him time while Ganesha considers them. This reverses the carried short cut's assignment. The narrative is from the Mahābhārata, not the Bhāgavatam. Whether this episode is included in a particular critical edition requires a separate scholarly source; no interpolation claim is established here.

**Transformer attention:** [Vaswani et al., §§3.2–3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03762v7) distinguishes learned projection matrices from input-dependent Q/K/V representations and attention weights. Standard attention is dense, with relevant masks; multiple heads use learned projection subspaces. Weighting does not establish comprehension. Neither a past/present/future faculty map nor Mushika-as-sparse-attention follows from the paper; those are the author's proposed imagery.

**Nash equilibrium:** A technical use needs players, strategy sets and payoffs, with no beneficial unilateral deviation at the proposed equilibrium. Self-worth and net worth alone do not supply that game. Use an equilibrium metaphor, or specify and test an actual model. The three-body comparison is optional imagery; a claim about celestial dynamics supplies no proof about dignity, wealth or witness consciousness.

**Ghati units:** Verify the selected tradition's unit definition, origin and division method separately. A sunrise-based origin does not imply a variable interval. Equal units and unequal divisions of daylight must not be conflated. No blanket duration or sidereal/solar equivalence is established here. Decision Mirror assignments beyond the supplied middle correspondence remain editorial proposals pending canonical source reading.

**The Clearing:** The source offers a phenomenological observation and a six-rung proposed cascade. This integration has not reviewed the primary physiology literature. Each autonomic, postural, endocrine and neural link remains a research task. A measured hormone change would not by itself establish a cause of clarity or of the refractory period. 'Wanting goes offline' is the author's description, not a demonstrated shutdown. Rung 6 is a symbolic/speculative interpretation. Reflection after desire is neither a universal experience nor an automatic truth window.

**Prenatal enculturation:** Locate and review original studies of maternal-voice preference and prenatal prosodic exposure, checking each design and inference separately. DeCasper and Fifer (1980) is a starting reference to inspect, not a verified citation for every language/prosody claim. No such finding would establish transmission of a complete ancestral cosmology in utero.

**Hemispheres and stars:** Different hemispheres do not observe identical skies. Seasonal alignment, visible constellations, and astronomical traditions differ substantially. No claim that 'all cultures looked at the same stars' without qualification.

**Source labels:** Source notes occasionally mark ideas as 'established', 'supported', or 'L5'. These carry the epistemic status of the intake session that wrote them, not of reviewed literature. Each brief notes which claims still require primary source verification.
