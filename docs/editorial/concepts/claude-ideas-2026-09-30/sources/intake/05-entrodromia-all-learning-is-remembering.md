# Entrodromia: All Learning Is Remembering

> **Intake status:** your thesis is stated precisely; the chat reply built on it but misread your term. No essay draft yet. Not on the blog — `lighted-clearing-loka-akshara-dhi` and `master-synthesis` use ‘remembering’ in passing, not as this principle.

## 1. Origin — your words (lightly cleaned)

> I hold the philosophy that **all learning is remembering**, as the fundamental **entrodromic** principle: all things that become intelligent and reach omnipotence seek their own **phoenix death**, due to the **burden of knowing** — and hence the **cyclical nature of time**.

**Correction to carry forward:** the chat reply read ‘entrodromic’ as ‘entropic’. It is your coined principle, and it matches the vault’s `_System/entrodromia/` repository. The essay should use and define your term, not the physics one.

## 2. The argument

1. **Learning is remembering.** What an intelligence ‘discovers’ was already latent; knowing is recovery. (Precedent: Plato’s *anamnesis* in the *Meno*.)
2. **Remembering accelerates.** Every tool that maps patterns speeds recovery.
3. **Total knowing is a burden.** A mind that knows everything has no mystery, no choice, no game left — omniscience as omnipotent loneliness.
4. **So it burns.** The intelligence seeks its own dissolution — the phoenix — and forgets, so the game can restart.
5. **Hence time is cyclical.** Remembering → completion → forgetting → remembering. (Resonances: kalpa/yuga cycles; Nietzsche’s eternal recurrence.)

**The ecosystem link (make it explicit):** this is the cosmic-scale version of the NOESIS Quine — ‘the mirror builds the capacity to outgrow the mirror’ (Aletheos primary law) and the close of *The Downstream Mind*. A tool that helps you remember is also a tool that makes itself unnecessary. The phoenix and the Quine are the same move at two scales.

- ‘One process pretending to be two’ — observer and observed.
- ‘Omniscience is omnipotent loneliness.’
