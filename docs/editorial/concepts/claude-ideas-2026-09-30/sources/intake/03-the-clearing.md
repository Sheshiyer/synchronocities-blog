# The Clearing

## 1. Origin — your framing (lightly cleaned)

Then, zeroing in:

> Neurobiology as clearing mechanism — the refractory period functions like a forced meditation, stripping away dopamine-driven illusions. Explain the bridge from emotion → physiology → posture → biology → chemistry → neurotransmitters → morphogenetic field symmetry alignment.

**Naming correction to carry forward:** ‘will to meaning’ is **Viktor Frankl** (logotherapy). **Otto Rank**’s term is **will** — creative will, and the artist-type who turns life-anxiety into creation. The essay is stronger using both: Rank supplies the creative will that is freed; Frankl supplies the meaning it reaches for.

## 2. Core thesis

For a few minutes after orgasm, the body runs a natural experiment most meditators spend years trying to set up: the **wanting circuits go offline**. What you see in that window — about your choices, relationships, pursuits — is what your psyche values when desire isn’t narrating. The refractory period is a free, involuntary clearing; the practice is learning to *use* it (Rank) and to access the same state without it (tantra, pratyahara).

## 3. The cascade (gross → subtle)

1. **Emotion → physiology.** Arousal is sympathetic: heart rate up, breath shallow and fast, muscles loaded. At climax it flips — the parasympathetic floods in; grasping becomes release. Functionally close to pratyahara.
2. **Physiology → posture.** The body moves from spring-loaded extension to collapse: psoas unlocks, diaphragm drops, jaw releases — savasana by force. The proprioceptive loops sustaining ‘seeking’ dismantle.
3. **Posture → biology.** Tension and release alter tissue state and cellular signalling (mechanotransduction). Reich’s ‘character armour’ briefly loses reinforcement.
4. **Biology → chemistry.** Oxytocin surge; dopamine drops sharply; prolactin rises (the satiety/refractory signal); endorphins; possibly endocannabinoids.
5. **Chemistry → neurotransmitters.** The reward system (VTA, nucleus accumbens) quiets; prefrontal control regains the floor; amygdala threat-reading softens under oxytocin.
6. **Neurotransmitters → field symmetry.** *(Your top rung.)* Oscillations synchronise, heart rhythm becomes coherent, and — in morphogenetic language — the organism briefly realigns with its template.
