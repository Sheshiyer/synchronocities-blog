# The Self-Worth Equilibrium

> **Intake status:** L2 — thesis stated by you; the chat reply expanded it but no draft exists. It is the developed form of the Nov 2024 seed ‘How business evolves with the founder’s self-worth and the artist’s journey’. Not on the blog (no post mentions Nash, net worth, three-body or infinite game). The origin chat also built a ‘wealth dashboard’ artifact — a product idea, out of scope here.

## 1. Origin — your words (lightly cleaned)

> My central philosophical approach is that **self-worth and net worth are a Nash equilibrium solution.** The net worth you can ‘contain’ or hold is **directly proportional to the self-worth** connected to your inner dialogue and to holistic growth of the physical, emotional and spiritual self.
>
> Much like the famous **three-body problem**, the **witness cannot be predicted** — like the seasons in the TV show, and like the actual problem of gravitational pull.
>
> So the game-theory solution is **optimisation of the vision rather than of resources.**

## 2. The argument

1. **Capacity sets the ceiling.** You can only hold as much as your self-worth can contain. Push net worth past it and the system destabilises: self-sabotage, impostor loops, windfalls that evaporate.
2. **The reverse also holds.** Self-worth with no outer expression creates its own tension (spiritual bypass, material denial).
3. **So it is an equilibrium, not a ladder.** Each variable constrains the other; stable wealth lives where they meet.
4. **Three bodies, one unpredictable.** Physical/material, emotional/relational, spiritual/awareness. The witness acts on all three but cannot be forecast — the three-body problem has no general closed-form solution.
5. **Therefore optimise the vision.** Resource-maximising fails when the system is chaotic; clarifying the vision changes what every body is pulled toward.
6. **The founder case.** A company grows to the size of its founder’s self-worth, and the artist’s journey (Rank’s creative will — see note 03) is how that capacity expands.
