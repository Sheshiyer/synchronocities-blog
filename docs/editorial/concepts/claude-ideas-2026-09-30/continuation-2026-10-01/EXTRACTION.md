### 2.3 Unresolved question
What does **entrodromia mean precisely in the author's own terms?** The source gives:
- a *principle* ("all learning is remembering"),
- a *mechanism-sketch* (burden of knowing → self-dissolution → forgetting → remembering), and
- a *resonance set* (anamnesis, kalpa/yuga, eternal recurrence).

What the source does **not** give is a public one- or two-sentence definition that distinguishes
entrodromia from (i) Platonic anamnesis, (ii) Nietzschean recurrence, (iii) ordinary thermodynamic
entropy running in reverse, and (iv) the general cyclical-time motif in Dharmic cosmologies.

Secondary open question: **omnipotence or omniscience?** The thesis sentence uses "omnipotence";
the argument uses "omniscience". These are not equivalent and the choice shapes the essay's
mechanism — a system that knows everything versus a system that can do anything collapse for
different reasons.
