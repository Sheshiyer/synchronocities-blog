## 2 · Register and voice calibration

These calibrations apply to a future *Signal Essay* draft of the diptych, per `docs/VOICE.md` §Signal Essay and §Voice register per tier.

- **Baseline voice**: *anatomist who sees fractals*, *PubMed × Alex Grey*, direct, no hedging. Each section opens on its claim, not its setup.
- **Grounded tone from the voice guide** (`docs/VOICE.md`): applied in the places this
  outline marks as *philosophical proposal*, so the proposal lands as proposition, not pose.
- **Clinical-visionary balance**: when the essay cites prenatal prosody research, the prose
  stays clinical; when it names *revisable remembering*, the prose carries image. The two
  tones are deliberately placed in the same paragraph, not segregated.
- **Attribution**: cite authors and works (not "the vault"), per `docs/VOICE.md`. The
  intake notes' vault-side provenance is kept in a sidecar if ever used, not in the body.
- **Attribution**: natural attribution carries source distinctions in finished prose. Detailed authorship and evidence categories stay in private sidecars; avoid per-section process announcements.

## 3 · Future adaptation requirements (no dates, no queue)

The following are **conditional** rules for later adaptation if and when the author approves
the drafts. Nothing here moves anything into any queue; nothing commits to any publication.

### 3.1 Signal Essay (the diptych itself)
- Follows the §Signal Essay frontmatter contract in `docs/VOICE.md`: at least
  four `concepts`, `llm.cluster` set, `llm.summary` present, three to five
  `canonical_questions`, `hero.eyebrow` + `hero.subtitle`, `experience.theme` /
  `experience.rail` / `experience.density` set.
- `concepts` list includes **inherited sandbox**, **entrodromia**, **revisable remembering**
  (if adopted) and **choice architecture**.
- `related_posts` names **the-downstream-mind**, **active-inference-prediction-engine**,
  **the-source-code-has-authors**, and **introduction-to-tryambakam-noesis** at minimum
  (consistent with intake 04 and intake 05.
- `llm.cluster`: the existing `consciousness-architecture` value fits the diptych's content
  and is already in use (per `docs/VOICE.md`.
- Opens on the thesis sentence, not the setup (`docs/VOICE.md`.)))

### 3.2 Field Note version (if entrodromia alone takes the Field-Note tier the intake proposes)
- Follows §Field Note contract (`docs/VOICE.md`): at least one `concept`,
  `llm.cluster` required, `hero.eyebrow: "Field Note"` recommended, `experience.density:
  "standard"`.
- A Field Note **must** carry a lived anchor (dated, first-person observation) per
  `docs/VOICE.md` and `references/noesis-writer-meaning.md`. If no actual
  lived anchor exists, the piece is not written as a Field Note — the brief at
  `docs/editorial/concepts/claude-ideas-2026-09-30/ARTICLE-BRIEFS.md` is explicit on
  this. **This continuation offers no lived anchor; it does not supply one.** The
  Field-Note form remains an option the author may choose only if an actual observation
  exists.

### 3.3 Note (short-form site Note, if a Note surface is used)
- Carries the author's thesis sentence exact carried-note wording as the lead, with attribution to the chat
  note where it originated.
- Names entrodromia with its author-supplied public definition before deploying it.
- Links to the diptych as the long-form source; the Note is a lead, not a summary that
  replaces the essay.
- Carries no empirical sentence beyond what the diptych itself has already cited.

### 3.4 X/Twitter thread
- The intake 09 §4.2 *Truth-Bomb cut* format (`sources/intake/09-seeds-and-residue.md` lines
  74–75) is noted as *one* candidate thread shape, not a mandate. Use only if the author
  elects it; it has its own tonal costs.
- A thread adaptation carries a standalone argument, a useful distinction and its consequence. Choose length and brand signal for the finished copy; the older article byline is prior usage, not a universal account rule.

- No empirical claim is tweeted that is not already cited in the diptych.

### 3.5 Substack adaptation
- The Substack-series plan referenced in intake 09 §2.1
  (`sources/intake/09-seeds-and-residue.md` notes a separate planning doc created
  by another session. **Any Substack adaptation of this diptych must be reconciled against
  that plan before it is written.** This continuation performs no such reconciliation and
  does not propose a Substack position.
- Substack may use a standalone full essay or a teaser, depending on the selected purpose. Retain the argument and source limits in either form.)

### 3.6 Decoder / easter-egg layer
- Any decoder layer uses the existing registry at `src/content/posts/the-downstream-mind.md`
  as prior art and does **not** retrofit it. New easter-egg entries, if any, live in the

  pattern.
- The Humble Psychic Plumber decoder (intake 08-J) is **not** attached to the diptych; the
  intake note (`sources/intake/08-project-held-dump.md` attaches it to
