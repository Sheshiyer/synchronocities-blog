## Register and movement

The Anatomist Who Sees Fractals joins precise observation to imaginative scale. Grounded, direct and respectful-challenging writing can include intimacy, humour, doubt and discovery. Technical language, concrete images and symbolic inquiry earn their places through what they let a reader see.

An opening can begin with an object, encounter, observation, definition, question, technical fact or argument. Choose the beginning that belongs to this piece. Do not manufacture a contradiction to satisfy a signature.

Sentence and paragraph lengths follow the thought. Fragments, contrast and parallelism are available devices, with no target ratio. Recurring negation reframes, triad demonstrations and short-line chains across sections or pieces require contextual revision when they expose the template instead of developing the subject.

An ending may leave an image, consequence, uncertainty, question or earned observation. It may conclude an argument when that serves the genre. No prescribed aphorism stack, mantra, imperative, signature or three-sentence limit applies.

## Humour and technical play

The person may be implicated in the joke. Wordplay, self-deprecation, absurdity and engineering wit are legitimate when they belong to the subject and narrator. The older assertion that humour is “never joke humour” excluded actual examples and is withdrawn. Do not force a joke into every section or introduce internal audit machinery to perform distinctiveness.

Computing, physics, anatomy, geometry, metallurgy and cartography remain useful imaginative domains. Explain only as much mapping as the argument needs. A compiler metaphor can expose a relation without being a literal biological compiler. A body image need not become an anatomy claim. A speculative connection remains revisable; technical vocabulary cannot borrow scientific authority for it.

## House models and scale

Kha-Ba-La may organize a real relationship; it need not appear or be named. An intervention need not always concern a missing pole. Attribute the house reading honestly rather than assigning it to a traditional source.

Cross-scale movement is optional and may stop wherever the inquiry belongs. There is no compulsory progression from personal to interpersonal to systemic to cosmic. Demonstrate a consequential relation and its limits; do not assert identity merely because two domains share a convenient description.

Difficulty, disagreement, error and consequence can change an interpretation. Avoid compulsory trap naming or a “reframe of the reframe.” Do not turn the narrator into a diagnostician of the reader’s life without grounds.

## Vocabulary and technical density

Choose words for their actual meaning. Runtime, substrate, architecture, protocol, witness, groove, compiler and cartography are useful when precise. Method, practice, agency, awareness, structure, cycle, journey and community are not forbidden synonyms. Historical, quoted and technical uses require context; replacing a wellness term with engineering jargon does not establish substance.

Technical density follows subject and audience, without a one-to-three paragraph ratio or a two-sentence grounding quota. An observable consequence, concrete image, calculation or ordinary explanation may orient the reader. Code and equations need a real explanatory purpose and their own correctness, provenance and consequence checks.

## Contextual review

- Does this piece develop its actual subject and retain a distinct person or perspective where appropriate?
- Which image, connection or observation does useful work, and which sentence merely announces profundity?
- Is the rhythm earned by the thought, or repeated from neighbouring pieces?
- Are humour and technical play helping the reader see something?
- Are experience, tradition, metaphor, house interpretation and empirical mechanism distinguishable?
- Does the encounter or consequence leave room to revise an interpretation?
- Does the ending belong to this particular piece?

These questions supplement evidence and publication gates; they never replace them. The anti-dependency principle governs the handoff, not a compulsory closing tagline.
