# No revelation quota

The five-field log has room for “nothing clear” in the sensation column and “uncertain” in the account of a shift. A few records may show a recurrence. They may show differences. They may show that the form leaves out something important.

There is no quota of revelations to extract from a notebook.

If you try the form, one useful review question is: which detail became easier to distinguish, and which part of the situation did these columns fail to hold?

A method should remain open to correction by the experience it was meant to describe.
