# Platform Formats — Editorial Reference v2.18.0

Read [contemporary author direction](contemporary-author-direction.md). These are adaptable preparation aids, not rhetorical templates or verified current platform specifications. Confirm actual editor capabilities, account limits, community rules and delivery behavior in the authorized publishing workflow before submission. Nothing in this reference authorizes messages, subscriptions, uploads or publication.

## Across platforms

Choose audience, source contribution, genre and length for the piece. A supplementary article should contribute a distinct useful angle and link to the relevant source rather than merely compressing the same opening and ending. Preserve attribution and exact-copy review. No platform requires a pattern-interrupt hook, negation reframe, triad, protocol, numbered sections or signature mantra by house rule.

A reading invitation can follow naturally from the contribution. Do not insert brand language into every paragraph or let traffic goals displace the reader’s reason to care. Adaptation changes require a fresh source/claim and copy review.

## X Article

Develop one argument or inquiry with enough room for its actual material. Title, opening, sections, images and sources should serve that movement. Use the formatting supported by the target editor; confirm preview rather than treating historical Markdown or separator conventions as current platform guarantees.

A personal opening, technical observation, concrete image or question may be appropriate. The earlier preamble→core thesis→structural point→signature arrangement is historical, not compulsory. Retain a byline or relevant destination when useful; the three-line brand credo is optional and should not become the default ending.

## X Thread and single posts

Use as many connected posts as the material warrants within verified account limits. Each post should contribute something while making sense in the thread. A short observation, useful source excerpt with commentary, question or concrete consequence can stand alone.

Do not manufacture a broken assumption, structural inversion and quotable close for every post. Numbering, emoji and hashtags depend on the assignment and audience, not a voice signature. Verify character counts and the exact sequence before human publication review.

## Substack article and Note

Write the article around its own question, encounter or argument. It may be a field note, essay, source response, account of practice or companion to a longer blog article. Preserve humour, imagery and conceptual ambition when they belong to it.

The former problem→Kha-Ba-La reframe→embodied protocol→philosophical close sequence is withdrawn as a default. A practical intervention belongs only when requested, supported and appropriate to the subject. Do not invent timed practices or lived outcomes merely to produce a takeaway. A Note can offer one worthwhile observation or invitation without simulating a complete essay.

Use source-specific links and a proportionate invitation to continue reading. Verify whether the selected delivery also sends email or notifications; approval of one destination or payload does not silently approve another.

## Reddit contribution

Read the relevant community’s current rules and discussion context. Write as a participant with something substantive to contribute. Choose a descriptive title and use headings, citations or a brief summary only where they help this particular post.

Do not assume an evergreen table of subreddit registers or permission for promotion. Disclose relevant affiliation when needed, and include external links only as permitted and useful to the discussion. A thoughtful question may remain open. A branded signature, product pitch or recycled thread is not a substitute for participation.

## Blog revision

Use the owning repository’s content schema and preserve frontmatter byte-for-byte under Route C unless metadata changes are explicitly authorized. Do not transplant a historical sample schema, deprecated host, category list or tagline into an existing post.

Travel dates, publication dates and revision dates mean different things. Preserve existing metadata and source history; do not invent an experience date or silently backdate a revision. A dated note can be added only when appropriate and authorized. Keep distinct layouts and the existing subject’s genre.

## Outreach email

Only draft or send within explicit authorization. Ground the note in the recipient’s actual work, the specific relationship to the author’s inquiry and an honest reason for contact. Keep gratitude proportionate. Do not claim lived proof, influence, personal familiarity or implementation results without a source.

Length, links, signoff and any request follow the real relationship and purpose. The historical 400–800-word pitch and compulsory GitHub/domain/social/signature block are not required. Sending remains a separate authorized action.
