Temperance Engine is part of my work at Thoughtseed on local agent operation. This 15-second portrait motion study follows a task through seven phases, a routing handoff and a sequence of checks.

The counters and success states are animated examples. The pacing is the design question here: a phase has to remain on screen long enough to be read while the sequence keeps moving.

On a phone, can you follow the change of phase, or would fewer labels make the sequence clearer?
