## Current human direction

The author rejected AI-sounding copy, especially repeated “not this, but that” framing, and then asked for a full return to the earlier blogs and complete writer package. The [October 9 close reading](voice-reading-2026-10-09/READING.md) supplies calibration and source provenance. Current human direction overrides historical fingerprint and platform templates.

Preserve embodied observation, irreverent humour, technical imagination, symbolic inquiry and the author's evolving cosmology. Concrete objects can carry meaning without an explanatory gloss after every image. Technical language should reveal a consequential connection; rhythm and density should follow the subject and genre. Personal field notes, research essays, fiction, references and platform adaptations need different treatment. Retain supplied personality and productive uncertainty rather than flattening everything into careful explanatory prose.

Review manufactured contrasts, aphorisms assembled for quotability, repetitive short-line cadence, tidy motivational endings and forced branded vocabulary in context. A meaningful negation, short declaration, triad, metaphor or unusual term can belong in the work. These are not blanket banned devices, and zero pattern findings cannot certify voice. Do not impose a compulsory joke, scene, bodily anchor, scale tour or closing form on every piece.

Do not manufacture scenes, bodily sensations, memories, dialogue, lived results or certainty. Label constructed examples naturally. Earlier repository versions establish wording rather than independently verified autobiography; calibration extracts include later rewrites. Distinguish source evidence, metaphor and authored interpretation privately and through natural attribution. Ambitious original synthesis remains welcome without presenting its imaginative relations as established empirical mechanisms.

## Preparation and review

1. Read the assigned source and record its hash. Select subject, audience, genre and source contribution before drafting.
2. Read the writer skill and selected references. Preserve its traditional/empirical/house-model/synthesis/metaphor distinctions in the private claim trace.
3. Draft from the material. Avoid importing a template's rhetorical shape into every topic.
4. Read the entire piece aloud or sentence by sentence. Check paragraph development, repeated openings, contrast scaffolds, unsupported claims and endings; also check whether imagery, conceptual wit, human texture and discovery survive the revision. Judge metaphors by what they reveal, not their mere presence. Compare neighbouring drafts for repeated templates.
5. Run `python3 scripts/audit-editorial-voice.py --output /tmp/editorial-voice-audit.json` for corpus triage. Findings are review prompts, never an AI detector or a voice-fit score. Contextual human judgment remains necessary even with zero findings.
6. New calendar-runner packets must include `writer_review` with this contract ID, canonical skill version, `contract_sha256` of the exact current WRITER-CONTRACT.md bytes, exact public-copy hash, reviewer, source coverage and a clear review verdict. A changed contract requires a fresh writer review for new enqueue operations. A changed public payload requires a fresh review. An agent's editorial review does not authorize publication.
