## Current human direction

The author rejected AI-sounding copy, especially repeated “not this, but that” framing. This instruction overrides older fingerprint and platform examples that encourage negation reframes, inversion hooks, compulsory triads, short-line cadence or signature blocks.

Write the actual subject in connected prose. Develop an observation or argument through concrete details, meaningful consequences and source-grounded distinctions. Let paragraph length and rhythm follow the material. Use ordinary sentences when ordinary sentences say it well. Preserve distinctive language that the author actually supplied.

Remove manufactured contrasts, aphorisms assembled for quotability, repetitive one-sentence paragraphs, tidy motivational endings and forced branded vocabulary. Do not manufacture scenes, bodily sensations, memories, dialogue, lived results or certainty to make prose sound human. Label a constructed example naturally. A real technical negation, disagreement or qualification can be necessary; review its meaning rather than replacing words mechanically.

## Preparation and review

1. Read the assigned source and record its hash. Select subject, audience, genre and source contribution before drafting.
2. Read the writer skill and selected references. Preserve its traditional/empirical/house-model/synthesis/metaphor distinctions in the private claim trace.
3. Draft from the material. Avoid importing a template's rhetorical shape into every topic.
4. Read the entire piece aloud or sentence by sentence. Check paragraph development, repeated openings, contrast scaffolds, decorative metaphors, unsupported claims and endings. Compare neighbouring drafts for repeated templates.
5. Run `python3 scripts/audit-editorial-voice.py --output /tmp/editorial-voice-audit.json` for corpus triage. Findings are review prompts, never an AI detector or a voice-fit score. Contextual human judgment remains necessary even with zero findings.
6. New calendar-runner packets must include `writer_review` with this contract ID, canonical skill version, exact public-copy hash, reviewer, source coverage and a clear review verdict. A changed public payload requires a fresh review. An agent's editorial review does not authorize publication.
