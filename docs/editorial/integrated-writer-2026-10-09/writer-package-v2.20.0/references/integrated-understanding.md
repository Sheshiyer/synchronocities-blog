## Understand the thing, then find its form

Build a connected understanding of the subject before choosing a rhetorical shape. A chronology can explain how an idea arrived, but arrival dates cannot supply its meaning. A concept becomes usable when the writer understands what it distinguishes, what it relates, which sense of its words is active, what follows from it and where it stops working.

Select the relevant material rather than feeding the entire archive into every article. Recover the source's question, premises, characteristic examples and argument. Reconstruct an idea in ordinary language; then use its precise or unusual vocabulary where that vocabulary contributes something ordinary language loses. If the reconstruction changes what the source actually claims, fix the understanding before polishing the sentence.

For original synthesis, make a compact private conceptual brief in the form most useful for this assignment:

- The actual question and the author's present contribution; distinguish supplied experience from an open conceptual question.
- Relevant concepts and their operative senses; source locators and actual reading depth.
- Relations: what depends on what, which comparison is proposed, what is being distinguished, and what cannot yet be reconciled.
- The transformation: how these ingredients produce a new distinction, relation, question or possibility, with authorship retained by the new work.
- A counter-reading or non-fit that could change the contribution; an actual consequence if recorded, otherwise a proposed return left pending.

These are understanding checks, not mandatory article sections or a demand that every Note invent a new cosmology. An adaptation can carry forward an existing contribution. Operational reporting can organise verified state and a consequential next action. A philosophical essay can explore a question before a lived return exists. Do not invent an encounter to complete the brief.

The author's scale and encounter axes remain independent tools. Use the relevant differences without requiring a tour of every scale or an equal visit to every quadrant. The situated mean is an interpretive centre when no commensurable coordinates exist; decorative calculus cannot calculate understanding. Dates, predecessors and adoption evidence belong in provenance. A historical essay may need chronology; a new essay need not perform the history of the author's worldview.

## Terms have senses and relationships

A lexicon is more than a list of preferred replacements. For each consequential term retain its sense, source/context, neighbouring concepts, distinguishing boundary and role in this argument. A term can carry more than one sense across a source; record the shift rather than forcing a single global definition.

For example, Young distinguishes the liquid metal Mercury, the messenger figure, the alchemical principle and the planet in *A Fool's Wisdom*, OCR PAGE151. In the Three Primes discussion (PAGE204–206), Mercury carries receptive/mind language and also spirit/liquor language in the herbal application. Those contexts do not establish that mind, alcohol, a planet and a metal are literally identical. A new essay can explore mediation across those senses while owning the comparison.

The author's Kha–Ba–La vocabulary provides a different working relation: witnessing/authorship, embodied carrier and formative resistance. Compare what each framework makes visible before proposing a mapping. Shared three-part structure is insufficient for equivalence. Preserve a source's distinction between soul and spirit when it matters. Replacing both with a single technical word can erase the very question the author is trying to examine.

Words such as God, soul, spirit, energy, witness, field, materialism and alchemy may belong to theological, experiential, historical, scientific or authored symbolic registers. Select the actual sense. Do not automatically lower spiritual meaning into biology, or raise a feeling into a universal physical law. Young is primary for his own account; he is not the sole authority for every alchemical tradition, scientific claim or the author's worldview. ## Why the anti-lists exist

Vocabulary cautions protect meaning and the reader's agency. They should identify the faulty move rather than punish a syllable. Preserve historical rules as provenance, but apply current human direction when an older rule would distort the subject.

| Concern | What the caution protects | Contextual treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Repeated “not X but Y” | A real distinction can be replaced by a rehearsed reveal; X becomes a straw man and Y borrows unearned certainty | Identify the exact assumption, evaluate it, keep necessary negation; remove interchangeable reversals |
| Repeated triads, scale tours, aphorism stacks | Formatting can pretend to be an explanation | Keep the actual three-part relation or useful scale change; remove the compulsory demonstration |
| Healing, transformation, awakening | A description can become a diagnosis, a promise or a dependency pitch | Preserve sourced, experiential, fictional and philosophical senses; verify any health effect separately |
| Energy, vibration, field, quantum | A term can slide from felt quality or metaphor into physical authority | Name the active sense and support any measurable mechanism; a clearly alchemical sense need not be given invented units |
| Truth, higher self, authentic self | The writer can install its own spiritual verdict over the reader | Develop the philosophical or source-specific meaning; avoid selling a predetermined identity |
| Optimisation, productivity, hacks | A generic performance frame can displace the actual purpose | Use when the technical or human question genuinely concerns those things, with its scope intact |
| Community, journey, path | Stock belonging and transformation language can substitute for a particular relationship or event | Name the real relation, travel or framework; retain the ordinary word when it is the precise one |
| Automatic technical recoding | Scientific vocabulary can alter meaning while appearing more rigorous | Compare meanings before substitution; death is not generally apoptosis, and soul is not automatically a bioelectric field |

This table creates no blanket device bans, quotas or universal semantic definitions. Deprecated product naming follows the active assignment; historical quotations retain their provenance. A source's prejudicial rhetoric, unsupported accusations or medical prescriptions do not become the author's voice through influence. Read what the source actually does, select the contribution, and apply the existing covenant and claim gates.

## Neti-neti has work to do

In this author's working inquiry, purposeful negation questions an identification or assumption. Name the target and the grounds for loosening it. Let the inquiry reveal what remains, including uncertainty. The writer need not instantly fill that space with a replacement belief or a new branded noun.

A limited report does not settle an ontology. A failure to recognise oneself in a label can loosen the label's hold without proving an eternal essence. A failed explanation can reopen a question without proving the first alternative. A necessary correction can use contrast; a chain of interchangeable revelations should be revised. Specific accounts of neti-neti in a tradition require their own source, rather than projecting this editorial practice onto all Vedanta.

## Compose with semantic fidelity

Keep public prose inside the inquiry. Images, humour, technical imagination and source language can carry the thought. Attribution should orient the reader where a source contribution enters. Place a meaningful boundary where the argument crosses registers; repeating caveats after every image can become another house style.

Section review is provisional. After assembly, read the entire output as a reader, then compare it with the whole selected source. Follow the subject, actors and pronouns, causal direction, temporal scope, modality and source ownership across paragraphs. Check that a local edit has not made the next paragraph answer a deleted question, repeat the same thought or contradict the opening. Verify that a metaphor has not hardened into mechanism downstream and that source paraphrase has not become author adoption in the conclusion.

Record a plain-language assessment of the contribution and what remains uncertain. An automated pattern count or invented voice percentage cannot perform this review. A sentence can pass every keyword scan and still say the wrong thing. Read neighbouring outputs for repeated semantic moves as well as repeated strings. Preserve the distinct thought and register of an essay, a short social contribution and an operational report.

Return the complete candidate with exact-copy evidence and unclosed questions. Editorial coherence is not human approval, author adoption or empirical verification. The existing lifecycle and publication review remain unchanged.
