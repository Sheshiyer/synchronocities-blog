# Cosmology evolution and source lexicon

Effective October 9, 2026, v2.20.0. Read for worldview, philosophical vocabulary, alchemical source work and revisions across time. This extends the existing living-cosmology method and contemporary author direction; Albedo, the covenant and publication authority retain precedence.

## Author direction and source ownership

The author asks for an evolving system that records cosmology, philosophy, worldview, perspective and words left behind, using Steven A. Young’s own writing as a primary source for alchemical vocabulary. This establishes a source priority and a method. Specific physical propositions and source definitions become author canon only through a separate, explicit author decision.

Preserve three voices: what Young says; what the writer proposes it could mean here; what the author chooses to adopt. A generated dossier or an old article establishes a document’s wording, not a dated human belief. Do not automatically recode soul, spirit or God into bioelectric coherence, fields or architecture. Such substitutions can alter meaning and introduce a scientific assertion. Read the source sense first; retain, adapt or decline it consciously.

The source-linked seed package is `/Volumes/madara/2026/Projects/tryambakam-noesis/synchronocities-blog/docs/editorial/cosmology-evolution-2026-10-09/`. It contains source cards, dated evolution events, a proposed lexicon, contextual vocabulary policies and a merge design. JSON record statuses there are editorial metadata; they do not extend or replace Albedo claim modes/statuses or artifact lifecycle states.

## Neti-neti and purposeful negation

For this author’s working practice, neti-neti can expose and relinquish an identification, inherited assumption or explanatory shortcut. Record what is being questioned, why it no longer holds, what remains useful and what is unresolved. An open remainder is a valid outcome. Do not force a positive replacement thesis, a polished contrast or a new house term to fill the opening.

This editorial direction does not establish one universal interpretation of Vedanta. Attribute specific traditional formulations to their own sources. Review “not X but Y” by its work in the argument: a real distinction, a necessary correction and purposeful philosophical negation can remain. Repeated interchangeable contrast is a revision prompt. Syntax alone cannot determine authenticity, philosophical depth or AI authorship.

## Conceptual integration before provenance chronology

Read [integrated understanding](integrated-understanding.md) as the composition method and [Young source understanding](young-source-understanding.md) for the full-book sense map and coverage limits. Retrieve concepts by the question, active sense, relationships, source contribution and unresolved difference. A lexicon records relationships and sense shifts, not just equivalent words. Recover what a source means before proposing what its concept can do in the author's cosmology. Current adopted positions guide authorial assertions; proposals remain exploratory. No chronological encounter sequence or adoption ledger is a mandatory public article structure.

## Dated evolution records — provenance

For each proposed shift record: ID, dimension, source IDs/locators, recorded date, effective date if known, earlier wording and its provenance, proposed/current wording, negated assumption, retained meaning, open question, adoption evidence and predecessor IDs. Separate publication date, document date, experience date, recorded date and author adoption date. Use null when unknown. Never infer belief dates from ingestion, Git timestamps or generated dossier metadata.

Append successor events; preserve prior records and source bytes. Historical wording may be valuable even when the author has moved on. Keep three editorial modes explicit: as-written historical review; present-day reflection on the earlier text; proposed new edition. No mode silently rewrites the original or transfers publication approval.

## Contextual vocabulary policies

Maintain term-plus-sense records with the reason for concern, relevant contexts, exceptions, historical scope, source and decision state. Distinguish a physical quantity, felt description, alchemical principle and metaphor when a word such as energy moves between them. Distinguish materialism as a specified ontological premise from a material description or practical use of matter. Do not infer a complete alternative cosmology from criticism of one premise.

A source lexicon needs a direct locator, reading depth and a separate proposed author definition. Video introductions do not establish full-talk definitions. OCR quotations require original-page verification. Young is primary for his own account; the history of an older tradition and the validity of empirical claims need their own evidence. Do not equate correspondence with identical mechanisms or borrow scientific authority through a technical synonym.

## Review and merge

Before drafting, select relevant concepts and source passages for the genre, subject and inquiry; consult dates for provenance and the chosen temporal mode. Current author-adopted positions guide authorial assertions. Proposed records may supply attributed source discussion or explicit exploratory synthesis, never silently adopted canon. Retrieve open disagreements as open disagreements.

For a merge proposal present the before/after proposition, source locators and coverage, remaining uncertainty, affected skills/articles/drafts and an exact diff. Ask only decisions that materially affect meaning: retain the source sense, adapt it with a stated difference, hold it open, or decline it. Author adoption and empirical warrant are independent. Use the existing Albedo grammar for every resulting assertion.

Record each decision with the exact proposal hash, actor, time and scope. Changed wording requires a new review. Accepted canon updates receive a successor event, versioned skill/reference update and mirror verification. Article revisions remain separate candidates subject to exact-copy publication review. Rejected or deferred proposals remain in history. No automatic majority vote among Muses can adopt author belief.

The October 9 package implements a local documentary workflow. It does not implement a remote review UI, background retrieval service, automatic semantic merge or publishing adapter. Future HITL integration must enforce these boundaries server-side and verify the actual approval round trip before claiming runtime acceptance.
