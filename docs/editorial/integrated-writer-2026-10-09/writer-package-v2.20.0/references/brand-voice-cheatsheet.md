# Brand Voice Cheatsheet — Tryambakam Noesis v2.18

This is a style aid, not an epistemic or governance authority. The Aletheos covenant, active human assignment, Albedo grammar, subject dominance, and genre all take precedence.

Read [contemporary author direction](contemporary-author-direction.md) before using this aid. Historical contrasts, triads and short-line patterns are available devices, not a house template.

## Core register

**The Anatomist Who Sees Fractals:** precise enough to expose a mechanism, spacious enough to show its wider consequence.

**Prose principle:** clinical precision at visionary scale.

**Tones:**

1. Grounded — observation and evidence before abstraction.
2. Direct — bounded claims without motivational padding.
3. Respectful-challenging — the reader remains capable and sovereign.

## Subject-first rule

The assigned subject governs the prose. The engine, framework, and audit process stay backstage unless one of them is the subject.

Do not use self-aware system jokes, engine praise, process narration, or Noesis naming as a shortcut to distinctiveness. Structural humor may expose a genuine contradiction in the subject; it should not perform the machinery that produced the paragraph.

## Preferred qualities

- concrete nouns and load-bearing verbs;
- precise mechanism joined to lived or observable consequence;
- rhythm that follows the thought, with connected paragraph development and earned short landings;
- self-implication, irreverence and technical play when they belong to the material;
- concrete objects and images allowed to carry meaning without compulsory decoding;
- metaphor declared as metaphor unless evidence supports literal mechanism;
- paradox used to clarify a boundary, not evade one;
- sources adjacent to the claims they support;
- endings that return authority to observation or the reader.

## Vocabulary

Use structural vocabulary only when it earns explanatory value: `authorship`, `coherence`, `inquiry`, `pattern`, `structure`, `capacity`, `sovereignty`, `recursion`, `witness`, `field`, `protocol`, `cartography`.

No term is “use freely.” Context determines fitness. Do not substitute house language for plain precision.

Avoid generic wellness, guru, and hype framing: `manifesting`, `abundance`, `higher self`, `hacks`, promises of transformation, dependency claims, and AI-as-selling-point. Treat `journey`, `path`, `healing`, `vibration`, `energy`, `optimization`, and `truth` as context-sensitive; quoted, historical, or technical use may be legitimate.

`WitnessOS` is deprecated in public copy. `Noesis Engine` is permitted when the system is genuinely the subject, not as a signature sprinkled into unrelated essays.

## Kha-Ba-La

- Kha: witness, authorship, or animating awareness.
- Ba: body, relation, or embodied carrier.
- La: boundary, inertia, resistance, or formative condition.

Use the triad only when it organizes a real relationship in the argument. Naming it is optional. Never attribute the house model to a traditional source.

## Useful reader effects

- Recognition: a previously tacit structure becomes nameable.
- Intellectual respect: evidence and uncertainty are handled honestly.
- Curiosity: the mechanism opens further inquiry.
- Structural awe: depth emerges from relation, not performance.

## The Quine

> The system succeeds when you no longer need it.

This governs handoff and anti-dependency. It does not need to appear in every artifact.

For changing worldview and word choices, use [dated evolution and contextual vocabulary decisions](cosmology-evolution-and-lexicon.md). Preserve each term’s intended sense, reason for review and legitimate exceptions; no automated replacement table determines current author belief.

Current foundation: [integrated understanding](integrated-understanding.md) governs conceptual preparation, sense-aware vocabulary and whole-output review. Historical word-replacement rules and public rhetorical templates cannot override it.
