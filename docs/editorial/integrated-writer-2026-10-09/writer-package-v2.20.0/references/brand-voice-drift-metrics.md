# Contextual brand, subject and epistemic review — v2.20.0

This reference supersedes v2.4's additive voice percentages and numeric thresholds. The historical changelog retains their provenance. A percentage cannot establish source coverage, semantic fidelity or a person's voice. The existing substantive gates remain fail-closed; this update changes the review representation, not consent, Albedo or publication authority.

Read [integrated understanding](integrated-understanding.md) and [contemporary author direction](contemporary-author-direction.md). Select the route and genre before applying stylistic expectations.

## Gate order and evidence

Review consent and safety, subject dominance/public process leakage, source/Albedo coverage, whole-output meaning, argument function, then genre and voice. Keep optional house vocabulary last. Record each applicable gate with an outcome, concrete evidence and any unresolved finding. A passing voice assessment cannot compensate for an unsupported claim or disclosure failure. An unresolved blocking gate retains QUARANTINED under the existing lifecycle.

## Subject dominance

For subject-first work, a cold reader should be able to describe the inquiry without mentioning the engine that prepared it. Find paragraphs that perform the system or brand instead of developing the topic. In system-subject or operational work, necessary system description is useful; self-validation, fabricated personhood, private topology and self-authorization remain failures. Naming Noesis alone establishes neither success nor failure.

## Vocabulary and process leakage

Assess words by their sense and argumentative role. Generic wellness promises, performance hype, implied diagnoses and dependency pitches are faults of meaning. Spiritual, philosophical, experiential or alchemical senses are available when they are honest and relevant; scientific claims require scientific support. Do not exchange healing for metabolism, soul for circuitry or death for apoptosis merely to look precise. Preserve historical terminology in attributed context and follow the active assignment's product naming.

Public prose should carry source ownership and necessary uncertainty. Internal paths, ledgers, prompts and claims of an engine's superior understanding usually displace the subject. A technical report may discuss a review process because it is the topic; that is different from leaking a private audit into a philosophical essay. Check context rather than treating every regex hit as a verdict.

## Epistemic integrity

Use [Albedo](albedo-epistemic-grammar.md) unchanged. Split material assertions, match source strength, trace synthesis and mathematics, compare blind source extraction with the ledger, and preserve observations separately from interpretations. Source attribution verifies what the source says; it does not establish the source's empirical claim. No topic watch-list, citation count or numerical average decides the claim's status.

## Whole-output meaning

Read the full selected source and full candidate. Check whether the actual question and semantic contribution survived. Follow actors/pronouns, causal direction, temporality, modality, term senses, comparisons and the source-to-author transition through the assembled work. Look for repetitions, dangling transitions and conclusions that claim more than the body supports. After a repair, reread the affected span with its neighbours and recheck later dependencies. Record the scope honestly: a sampled opening/end review is not a full-body comparison.

## Genre and voice

Explain what the writing lets the reader see. Preserve useful concrete images, technical imagination, human texture, irreverence, discovery and uncertainty when they belong to the source and genre. A report may be plain; a philosophical essay can remain dense and exploratory; a short social observation can end before becoming a complete essay. No compulsory scene, joke, scale tour, three-part model or landing applies.

Assess repeated manufactured contrasts, aphorism stacks, fragment chains and motivational closure in context. Necessary negation, a genuine triad, an earned short sentence and a sourced philosophical term may remain. Keep the specific source's voice alive through faithful adaptation rather than copying its phrasing or importing generic professional neutrality.

## Cross-piece review and disposition

When more than five related pieces are produced, retain the existing delayed cross-piece review before promotion. Compare semantic shapes as well as strings: repeated straw-man reveals, caveat chains, identical demonstrations and interchangeable conclusions. If a recurring scaffold materially damages the work, identify affected pieces and keep the batch held for contextual repair and human review. An isolated repeated connective or intentional article/thread adaptation is not automatically contamination.

Return concrete findings, changes, source limits and candidate state. A complete editorial pass remains DRAFT pending the existing human publication decision; failing gates remain QUARANTINED. No agent-generated assessment confers HUMAN-APPROVED. Repository validators and scans supply evidence for their actual scope, never a numerical certificate of authorship, truth or literary quality.
