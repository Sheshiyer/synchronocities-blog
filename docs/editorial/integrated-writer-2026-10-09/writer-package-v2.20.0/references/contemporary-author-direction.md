## The voice to recover

The Anatomist Who Sees Fractals combines close observation, technical imagination, irreverence and symbolic inquiry. Clinical precision can coexist with psychedelic scale, humour and uncertainty. Removing obvious model habits is only part of the work; a polished paragraph that could belong to any professional newsletter can still miss this voice.

- **A person remains visible.** Self-implication and absurdity can carry intimacy. “A Vedic sage with an overclocked sound card” followed by “imposter syndrome with good taste” places the narrator inside the joke. This is an example of personality, not a formula to reproduce.
- **Objects carry meaning.** The earlier Arrival lets a door number, bag, cards, flame and breath do some of the thinking. Explain a relation where the reader needs it; leave room for an image to work without decoding every object into a framework.
- **Technical language participates.** “Markdown does not speak to muscle memory. Breath ratios do not serialize to JSON” exposes an incompatibility between documenting practice and inhabiting it. Computing, anatomy, geometry and ritual may meet when the connection reveals something specific. A technical source domain does not validate the target claim.
- **Rhythm has range.** Longer movement can earn a short landing. Paragraph development follows attention and argument. Avoid industrial repetition of negation reframes, triads and punchline fragments across pieces. No minimum short-line ratio, maximum closing length or compulsory opening device applies.
- **Inquiry can change its mind.** An encounter or consequence may revise the interpretation brought to it. Living cosmology remains open to action, recurrence, error, consequence and reconsideration. Keep house interpretation, tradition, observation and empirical mechanism distinguishable while allowing ambitious synthesis.

Different genres require different densities. Personal field notes, research essays, fiction, references and short social posts cannot share one rhetorical chassis. There is no quota for scenes, jokes, scales, metaphors or bodily language. Preserve truthful supplied experience; never fabricate autobiography or dialogue to make a piece sound lived.

## Review practice

Read the whole source before revising. Record its hash and preserve frontmatter under Route C. Identify what already works: a concrete image, a consequential connection, a distinctive phrase, an honest uncertainty or an encounter that moves the argument. Keep it unless another requirement compels a change.

Review recurring rhetoric in context. A real disagreement can require contrast; a genuine relation can have three parts; a short sentence can land. Remove scaffolding when it substitutes for thought. Do not mechanically invert the ban into another template, simplify away all technical imagination, or replace a distinct narrator with generic reassuring exposition.

Read adjacent drafts together to catch copied openings, interchangeable conclusions and repeated explanatory moves. Automated scans aid triage and cannot certify voice fit or identify AI authorship. Preserve an exact revision trace and claim map. Revised candidates require their own exact-copy human review; earlier approvals never transfer silently.
