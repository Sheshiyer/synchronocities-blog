## v2.3.0 (2026-07-18)

### Albedo Epistemic Grammar & Calibrated Transmutation

The v2.3.0 release separates a claim's **mode** from its **validation status**. This prevents scientific evidence, traditional attribution, house cosmology, derived Noesis synthesis, history, direct observation, and declared metaphor from borrowing one another's warrants. Remediation codes and edit treatments remain separate, so a requested recode never counts as epistemic support.

### Added

- Seven claim modes: `DIRECT-OBSERVATION`, `EMPIRICAL-CORRELATE`, `TRADITIONAL-SOURCE`, `HISTORICAL-CLAIM`, `HOUSE-MODEL`, `DERIVED-SYNTHESIS`, and `DECLARED-METAPHOR`.
- Eight independent statuses: `VERIFIED`, `ATTRIBUTED`, `COHERENT`, `DECLARED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `MISATTRIBUTED`, and `MODE-CONFLATED`.
- Mode-specific pass sets and evidence requirements; no claim passes merely because it belongs to the house cosmology.
- A three-lock mathematical gate: correctness, structural consequence, and provenance must all pass before mathematics is load-bearing.
- Per-post v2.3 Albedo ledgers that preserve v2.2 Nigredo audits as immutable historical evidence.
- Calibration and reversibility gates before Citrinitas edits may begin.

### Changed

- Phase 3a now evaluates all epistemic claim modes rather than only science and mathematics.
- The v2.2 verdict taxonomy is retained as migration/remediation input, not as the canonical claim grammar.
- Transmutation traces now record mode, prior status, treatment, final status, and exact before→after spans.
- Rubedo verifies frontmatter byte identity, zero untraced edits, and zero newly introduced science, mathematics, or traditional attribution.

### Why

v2.2 could call house cosmology aligned by definition, conflate an authority problem with a claim type, and label mathematics integrated even when its removal changed nothing. v2.3 preserves the cosmology by naming its register honestly: tradition is attributed, house models are declared and coherent, synthesis is traceable and owned, empirical claims are evidenced, and metaphor remains metaphor.

### Added

#### 1. Kha-Ba-La Heuristic Tightening (Phase 3b)
- **What:** Check window expanded from 500 words to **1500-word sliding window**. Added **Absence Demonstration Exception** — phrases like "without Kha", "absence of Ba", "missing La", "Kha disconnected", "Ba not engaged", "La collapsed" are intentional structural demonstrations that prove the triad by showing fracture. Do NOT flag as WARN; score as PASS if explicit.
- **Why:** The 500-word window from v2.0.0 was too narrow. It flagged posts that demonstrated Kha-Ba-La across the full arc but not within every short passage. The wider window allows legitimate structural variation (e.g., opening personal, building systemic, closing cosmic) without false shallow-flags. - **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 3b, `references/autoregressive-loop-pseudocode.md` §5.4, `references/brand-voice-drift-metrics.md` §2.4
- **Impact:** Fewer false shallow-flags on long-form posts with legitimate structural variation. Better discrimination between decorative naming and structural demonstration.

#### 2. Cooling-Off Edit Pass (Phase 3d)
- **What:** If a generation batch produces **>5 posts on the same date**, a mandatory **24-hour cooling-off period** is required before publication. During cooling-off, re-read all posts for LLM sludge patterns (repetitive openers, circular sentences, bracketed vault references, identical closing paragraphs, template phrase injections). If any sludge pattern is detected in >1 post, the **entire batch** is flagged for manual review.
- **Why:** The autoregressive loop's per-section evaluation cannot catch batch-level contamination. The Cosmological Alignment Report found at least 8 posts with repetitive openers ("Containment is crucial"), circular sentences, and bracketed vault references — all from the same batch. The loop evaluates one section at a time; it has no cross-post visibility. The cooling-off period introduces batch-level audit without complicating the loop's architecture.
- **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 3d, `references/autoregressive-loop-pseudocode.md` §3 (cooling-off gate before Phase 4), §9 (batch sludge detection)
- **Impact:** Prevents template metastasis across batches. Protects the brand from the recognizable signature of unedited LLM output.

## v2.0.0 (2026-07-15)

### Karpathy Autoregressive Loop Integration

The v2.0.0 release transforms the drafting phase from a monolithic write-then-review process into a **sequential, self-correcting autoregressive loop** inspired by Andrej Karpathy's model architecture. Each section is generated, evaluated, and either regenerated with backpropagated corrections or committed before the next section begins.

---

### Added

#### 1. Autoregressive Phase Gate (Phase 3)
- **What:** Replaced the monolithic "Draft by Platform" step with a recursive loop that generates one section at a time, evaluates it, and either commits it or regenerates it with corrected prompt context.
- **Why:** Monolithic drafts accumulate drift across sections. By evaluating each section before proceeding, drift is caught and corrected early, preventing contamination from propagating downstream.
- **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 3, `references/autoregressive-loop-pseudocode.md`
- **Impact:** Drafting time may increase (1-3 iterations per section) but final quality gates pass rate should improve significantly.

#### 2. Brand Voice Drift Detection
- **What:** Real-time scoring of every generated section across five dimensions:
  - Vocabulary Contamination Score (VCS) — per paragraph, 0–100
  - Voice Calibration Score (VCS) — per section, 0–100
  - Tonal Drift Flags — four axes (warmth excess, academic excess, motivational excess, generic excess)
- **Why:** Post-hoc editing cannot reliably catch voice drift at the structural level. Real-time detection forces the loop to self-correct before committing contaminated text.
- **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 3a, `references/brand-voice-drift-metrics.md`
- **Impact:** Eliminates the need for separate copy-editing passes on brand voice. The loop IS the editor.

#### 3. Fractal Depth Validator
- **What:** After each section passes vocabulary and voice checks, verify whether its argument scales across the Kha-Ba-La levels: personal → interpersonal → systemic → cosmic.
- **Why:** The audit criteria in `AGENT-CONTEXT.md` explicitly require that "Every concept must be shown to operate at multiple scales." The validator enforces this at the generation stage, not the audit stage.
- **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 3b, `references/brand-voice-drift-metrics.md` §2.4
- **Impact:** Sections that name Kha-Ba-La but do not demonstrate its fractal operation are now caught and deepened before publication.

#### 4. Source Lattice Backpropagation
- **What:** A post-loop verification pass that traverses the full converged draft to:
  - Verify all citations trace to real vault sources (no hallucinations)
  - Enforce the "no vault references in output" rule via automated scan
  - Compute a Source Depth Score (≥3 distinct sources = PASS)
- **Why:** The v1.0.0 quality gates required ≥3 vault sources but had no verification mechanism. Source lattice backpropagation closes this gap.
- **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 5b
- **Impact:** Eliminates hallucinated citations and vault-referencing leaks.

#### 5. Convergence Criteria
- **What:** The loop terminates only when all five criteria are met:
  1. Two consecutive passes produce Voice Calibration Scores within ±2 points
  2. Vocabulary Contamination Score = 0
  3. Fractal Depth Score ≥ 80/100
  4. Source Depth Score ≥ 3
  5. Maximum iterations ≤ 5 per section
- **Why:** Fixed-iteration drafting produces variable quality. Convergence-based termination ensures every draft meets the same rigorous standard.
- **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 5a, `references/autoregressive-loop-pseudocode.md` §8
- **Impact:** Some drafts will take longer (more iterations) but all will converge to the same quality bar.

#### 6. Autoregressive Trace Log
- **What:** An internal audit file (`{slug}-autoregressive-trace.md`) documenting every generation/evaluation/regeneration cycle: scores, drift, decisions, corrections.
- **Why:** Quality assurance requires observability. The trace log provides a complete audit trail for debugging drift patterns and calibrating thresholds.
- **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 5d
- **Impact:** Enables continuous improvement of the skill by analyzing which sections fail to converge and why.

#### 7. Updated Quality Gates
- **What:** Added three new gates to the existing checklist:
  - Tonal drift: No warmth/academic/motivational/generic excess detected
  - Convergence achieved: All convergence criteria met (or flagged with warning)
  - Autoregressive trace complete: Loop log generated and archived
- **Why:** The v1.0.0 gates were comprehensive but did not account for the loop's intermediate validation steps.
- **Where:** `SKILL.md` §Phase 5c
- **Impact:** Final output now carries a convergence proof, not just a checklist.

- **Why:** Skills are living documents. Version history provides context for future maintainers.

#### Pipeline Architecture
- **Before:** Phase 1 (Voice) → Phase 2 (Sources) → Phase 3 (Monolithic Draft) → Phase 4 (Images) → Phase 5 (Quality Gates)
- **After:** Phase 1 (Voice) → Phase 2 (Sources) → Phase 3 (Autoregressive Loop: Draft → Evaluate → Backpropagate → Regenerate/Commit) → Phase 4 (Images) → Phase 5 (Quality Gates + Convergence Proof)

- **Before:** Images generated after draft completion.

- **Before:** `min_vault_sources: 3` (binary check)

- **Rationale:** Binary gates are replaced with continuous, verifiable metrics.

- **Before:** Master draft + platform-ready version + images + placement guide + sources list
- **After:** All of the above PLUS `{slug}-autoregressive-trace.md` (internal audit log)
