# The room around a choice

In the imagined work-request example, “Let me check the week before I answer” creates a small pause. But the pause still has conditions: time to check, permission to delay, and somewhere to record what has already been promised.

An alternative response deserves examination alongside the room available for it. What makes this action possible here? What makes it costly? Who else carries its consequences?

One quieter question to take from this week’s essay: what needs to change around the choice for the choice to become workable?
