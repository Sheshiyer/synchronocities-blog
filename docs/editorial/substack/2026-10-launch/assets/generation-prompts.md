# Image generation prompts

Tool: built-in image_gen. Two new images, opaque backgrounds. User authorized generation. No existing assets were edited or replaced. Intended roles: essay cover metaphors; no literal neurological diagram or efficacy illustration.

## 01 — Insight and next turn

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: landscape Substack essay cover for 'Why Insight Isn't Change'. Primary request: a restrained editorial image of the difference between recognizing a repeated path and making one small change. Scene: deep navy-black tabletop, a thin warm bronze thread worn into a looping track; a single deliberate turn leaves the loop and crosses a small open gap, with the original track still visible. Style: tactile fine-art editorial illustration, subtly engraved linework and real paper/fiber textures, precise and contemplative rather than fantasy. Composition: wide landscape 16:9, central motif legible at thumbnail size, generous space, no borders. Lighting: warm side light on bronze thread, quiet deep violet shadow. Palette: void navy #070B1D, muted bronze gold #C5A017, parchment #F0EDE3, small indigo accent. Constraints: metaphorical object scene, no brain, neurons, medical claims, sacred symbols, faces, text, labels, watermark or logo. The single changed turn must be visible, not an explosive transformation.

## 02 — Record before explanation

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: landscape Substack essay cover for 'A Record Before an Explanation'. Primary request: an editorial still life showing careful observation separated from an explanatory story. Scene: open parchment notebook on a deep navy desk; five simple empty ruled columns visible, a modest bronze pen and an indigo thread tracing one quiet repeated curve alongside the page. Style: tactile fine-art editorial illustration with subtly engraved contours and paper texture. Composition: wide 16:9, top-down slightly oblique view, five clearly separated blank columns as the focal motif, no decorative occult symbols, no invented handwriting or words. Lighting: soft warm side light with restrained violet shadow. Palette: navy-black #070B1D, parchment #F0EDE3, bronze #C5A017, small indigo accent. Constraints: no readable text, labels, title, watermark, logo, diagrams of bodies or brains, mystical auras, numerical outcome promises; observed record metaphor only.
