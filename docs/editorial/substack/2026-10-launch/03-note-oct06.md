# The next request

You can explain a recurring response with considerable precision and still make the same choice when the next request arrives.

Try holding two sentences apart: “I understand why I agree,” and “I checked the week before I answered.” Each needs its own evidence.

For one ordinary, low-stakes situation, what would a different response actually look like? A question? A delayed answer? A conversation about conditions that a private resolution cannot change?

Thursday’s essay begins there: the distance between an account of a pattern and the next occurrence of it.
