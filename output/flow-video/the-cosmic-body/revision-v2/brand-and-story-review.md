## The article's visual argument

Mixture acquires a contour. A contour earns value through the movement it enables. Four distinct movements sustain one shared life. The whole remains larger than any function.

The hinge in the opening is useful because it can actually do something: it lets a door turn. An animated cavity without a resulting act stops the argument halfway. The four functions must similarly become recognizable through consequences, rather than through colored points on a body.

## Recommended direction — The dwelling inside the body

Use a designed, tactile architectural cutaway with engraved anatomical precision. Begin with a bronze hinge being cast, then follow that hinge into a doorway. Through that doorway, one shared interior contains the work of teaching, holding/protecting, provision/exchange and making. The final composition reveals their dependence through the same dwelling, with a restrained body contour tying its structure together.

The dwelling is an authored bridge from the article's hinge/door/dwelling image. It is not a claim that the hymn literally describes architecture. Its use is to make interdependence visible at a scale the viewer can recognize. Avoid a miniature city stacked by social rank or four people assigned hereditary identities. The functions are acts within shared life.

### Material and composition

Void Black ground; thin Parchment engraved detail; restrained Sacred Gold activation; a small Emerald resolution accent. Bronze appears at the hinge and structural joints, rather than coating every surface. Texture belongs to paper grain, metal, timber and worked material. Facial identity is absent; hands, forearms, mouths cropped below eyes, and feet supply situated action. These are designed cinematic scenes, never fabricated footage of the author.

Geometry carries a load: hinge pin, beam, arch, threshold and joint. No decorative mandala, wandering particle, ornamental constellations or floating cosmic scenery. No invented physiology labels. Typography is set in the finishing edit using actual Panchang/Satoshi, if needed; never rely on generated spelling.

### Editing and continuity

Keep one doorway, one hinge design, one axis of movement and one shared space. Match the hinge axis into the doorway; match the doorway opening into the final whole. Sound can carry continuity through metal contact, wood settling, grain movement and room resonance. No soundtrack or chant assumed before production.

## Pacing decision

Six eight-second beats (48 seconds) would let the four functions each receive a consequential action. This is the preferred story treatment, not approval for six paid generations:

1. **The cast:** molten material fills a hinge mold; the resulting hinge becomes legible. End on the pin axis.
2. **Transmission / mouth:** a teacher's finger guides another hand across a precise working drawing; the receiving hand continues the line independently. Knowledge becomes usable.
3. **Protection / arms:** forearms secure a structural brace at the doorway, allowing activity behind it to continue. Protection holds a condition.
4. **Exchange / thighs:** grain is poured from a full vessel into another person's waiting vessel within the same shared interior. Sustenance circulates.
5. **Execution / feet:** a craftsperson seated with feet grounded seats the fitted hinge pin; the door bears its weight and turns. Skilled contact makes the promise hold.
6. **One Person:** the door opens and a wider architectural composition reveals the connected workstations in one dwelling. A body contour emerges through the structure in the edit, with no dismemberment or ascending hierarchy.

These acts are cinematic constructions mapped to the essay's functions; the arm brace and grain transfer are not traditional varṇa iconography.

## Three-shot alternative under the original 24-second format

- **00–08 — Fit:** hinge casting and seating are presented through a deliberate match cut, ending at the closed doorway. Avoid asking one model clip to transform molten metal into finished hardware.
- **08–16 — Contribution:** four concise action inserts, two seconds each: line transmitted, brace secured, grain transferred, pin seated. Each insert must be legible before cutting. This requires an edited montage; a single generated clip should not be entrusted with four synchronized tableaux.
- **16–24 — Wholeness:** the same doorway turns on its fitted hinge, revealing the shared interior. The whole-body correspondence is established through controlled composition and a designed finishing layer.

This format is a teaser. It cannot carry the entire gaṇa–guṇa, kosha and witness argument in 24 seconds. Preserve those layers in the article; do not overload the film with three more symbolic diagrams.
