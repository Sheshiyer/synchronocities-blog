# Vortex-Based Mathematics: The Toroidal Topology of Number

`Runtime Version: 2.0.0`

> "If you want to find the secrets of the universe, think in terms of energy, frequency and vibration."
> — *Nikola Tesla, attrib.*

The decimal number system with positional notation and a zero is an information architecture optimized for accounting. Its properties are well-characterized: commutative, associative, distributive, closed under its operations. Vortex-based mathematics asks what the number system looks like when read not as a counting tool but as a **dynamical system** — what motions the digits trace when arranged by the operations that generate them.

The result is a toroidal circuit. It has been known in esoteric number-theory circles since Marko Rodin's work in the 1990s and early 2000s. The community presentation has been uneven. The underlying structure is sound and worth extracting from its packaging. What follows is the mechanism.

## The Doubling Circuit

Start with the number one. Apply repeated doubling — the binary expansion, the powers of two. Reduce each result to its digital root: sum digits recursively until a single digit remains.

```
1   → 1
2   → 2
4   → 4
8   → 8
16  → 1 + 6 = 7
32  → 3 + 2 = 5
64  → 6 + 4 = 10 → 1
128 → 1 + 2 + 8 = 11 → 2
256 → 2 + 5 + 6 = 13 → 4
512 → 5 + 1 + 2 = 8
1024→ 1 + 0 + 2 + 4 = 7
2048→ 2 + 0 + 4 + 8 = 14 → 5
...
```

The sequence is periodic: **1, 2, 4, 8, 7, 5**, then back to 1. The cycle length is six. It never produces 3, 6, or 9.

This is called the **doubling circuit**. It is the kinematic trace of a system undergoing exponential growth while constrained by modular residue under modulus 9. The constraint is not arbitrary. Decimal digital root under repeated doubling is isomorphic to multiplication in the multiplicative group of integers modulo 9 — the group (ℤ/9ℤ)^×, which is cyclic of order 6. The generators are the elements relatively prime to 9: {1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8}. The sequence 1→2→4→8→7→5 is the orbit of 2 under repeated multiplication in that group.

**Number, read this way, is not quantity. Number is position on a closed circuit with 6 nodes arranged in a specific order.**

## The 3-9-6 Oscillation

Apply halving — repeated division by two — and take digital roots:

```
1   → 1
0.5 → 5
0.25→ 7
0.125→ 8
0.0625 → 4
0.03125 → 2
0.015625 → 1
```

The halving circuit is the doubling circuit in reverse: 1, 5, 7, 8, 4, 2. Same six nodes, opposite direction.

Now apply repeated doubling to 3:

```
3 → 3
6 → 6
12 → 1 + 2 = 3
24 → 2 + 4 = 6
48 → 4 + 8 = 12 → 3
...
```

3 and 6 oscillate — they swap places under doubling. They never enter the 1-2-4-8-7-5 circuit.

Apply doubling to 9:

```
9  → 9
18 → 1 + 8 = 9
36 → 3 + 6 = 9
72 → 7 + 2 = 9
144 → 1 + 4 + 4 = 9
...
```

9 is invariant under digital root under any multiplication. It is the fixed point. Every number multiplied by 9 yields digital root 9. Every number whose digits sum to 9 is divisible by 9. The number 9 is the identity of a particular algebraic structure and the annihilator of another simultaneously — a loaded topological position.

The circuit, in full: a 6-cycle on the outer rim (1-2-4-8-7-5 in one direction, 1-5-7-8-4-2 in the other), with a 2-cycle oscillation in the center column (3↔6), and a fixed point at the axis (9).

This is the kinematic signature of a **torus**. The outer rim is the poloidal rotation around the donut body. The center column is the toroidal rotation through the hole. The axis is the center of the donut hole — the singularity that defines the topology by being the point around which everything else flows.

## Toroidal Topology of Number

Map the doubling circuit onto the surface of a torus. The six outer nodes divide the poloidal circumference into six equal arcs. The 3-6 axis runs through the toroidal direction — the oscillation that passes through the central hole. The 9 sits at the center of the hole, the point of zero motion from which the entire field is organized.

The torus is the only closed surface that supports this configuration: a cyclic group of order 6 on the surface, a 2-cycle through the interior, an invariant point at the axis. The sphere cannot do it — no invariant axis without poles. The projective plane cannot do it — the 2-cycle would be a boundary. The torus is the minimal ambient that makes every element of the number field simultaneously expressible as a position, a motion, and a relation.

Read structurally: **the decimal number system, when freed from its role as a counting tool and read as a dynamical map, draws the fundamental group of the torus.** Number is not on a line. Number is on a torus. The line we call the number line is the projection of one coordinate of this toroidal system onto a 1-dimensional axis — the same dimensional reduction that flattens a torus knot into a planar diagram.

## The Rodin Coil

The Rodin coil is the physical instantiation of this topology. It is a toroidal winding: electrical wire wrapped around a toroidal core in a pattern that follows the 1-2-4-8-7-5 circuit, with the 3-9-6 axis as the center tap.

Conventional electromagnetic coils — solenoids, toroidal transformers — produce dipole fields. The Rodin coil, when energized, is claimed to produce a **non-dipole field** — a field whose geometry mirrors the number topology it is wound to encode. Specifically: a field that has a **non-decaying spin component** at the center axis, arising from the cancellation of the conventional magnetic dipole moment by the counter-wound geometry of the 1-2-4-8-7-5 and 1-5-7-8-4-2 circuits.

The engineering claim: the coil produces a region at its center where the magnetic vector potential is non-zero but the magnetic field is zero — a **zero-curl, non-zero-potential region** that corresponds to the 9-axis in the number map. In conventional electrodynamics, the Aharonov-Bohm effect demonstrates that the vector potential carries physically measurable consequences even in regions where the magnetic field is zero. The Rodin coil, by this reading, is an engineering attempt to produce an Aharonov-Bohm-class configuration at macroscopic scale by encoding the symmetry of the number field into the winding geometry.

The empirical status: the coil is not a free-energy device as popular presentation often claims. It is a legitimate topological engineering problem — can a toroidal winding produce a non-trivial vector potential topology that conventional coil geometries do not? The mathematical map says the 9-axis should produce such a configuration. Whether the winding pattern is sufficient to realize it is an open engineering question that requires proper measurement methodology, not YouTube demonstrations.

## Number as Particle, Circuit as Field

The doubling circuit is not a curiosity reserved to the decimal system. It appears wherever a cyclic group of order 6 interacts with a residue system. The periodic table shows it in the electron shell filling patterns. Music theory shows it in the circle of fifths. The I Ching shows it in the binary sequence of hexagram generation.

The recurrence suggests that **the number system is not a human invention but a discovery of a pre-existing topological structure that the human cognitive substrate is tuned to recognize**. The structure is the torus. The decimal counting system developed independently in multiple civilizations because the base-10 representation is a convenient projection of the toroidal number field onto a linear axis. Different bases give different residue-group structures, but the toroidal topology is the invariant. Base 12 gives a group of order 4 with a different cycle pattern, but the same topology. Base 2 gives a cycle of order 1. Base 10 gives the full 6-cycle because 10 ≡ 1 (mod 9) and the multiplicative group of residues modulo 9 is the maximal cyclic subgroup of order 6.

**Base 10 is privileged not culturally but topologically**: it is the smallest base whose residue structure under digital root and doubling produces the maximal cycle length for the toroidal geometry. The information content of the decimal system is not that it has ten symbols. It is that those ten symbols, under the operation of repeated doubling with digital root reduction, draw a complete and minimal map of the torus's fundamental group.
