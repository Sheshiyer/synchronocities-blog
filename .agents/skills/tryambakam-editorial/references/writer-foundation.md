## Carry one meaning through the processes

The sequence is: **current writer foundation → source reading and selected context → complete manuscript or substantive contribution → channel adaptation → full-piece writer review → exact-item human publication review → existing delivery and receipt → learning returned as a proposal**.

Before composition, record the assignment’s subject, genre and temporal mode (`as-written`, `present-reflection` or `new-edition`). Record the current writer version and contract hash. Where relevant, keep selected evolution/lexicon/policy IDs, source locators, the author-adoption state and open questions with the existing private source/claim sheet. Explicitly mark an empty selection as not applicable; do not fill unrelated assignments with alchemical terms to demonstrate compliance.

Channel adaptations carry the source’s relevant proposition, distinctions and unresolved questions. Each receives its own structure, rhythm and exact-copy review. An X post may narrow the claim to fit its length; it must not erase the boundary that makes the claim honest. A Substack essay may develop a consequence; it must not invent a lived outcome. A Reddit contribution must serve the actual discussion and community rather than become a pasted promotional excerpt.

If an adaptation introduces a new philosophical proposition, term sense or empirical assertion, return that addition to source/claim review. Proposed canon stays proposed. Compare the complete adaptation with the manuscript and adjacent drafts before calling it ready for human review. Preserve humour, imagery and technical imagination where the material supports them.

## Apply at each entry point

- **Concept development and weekly/daily preparation:** load the current foundation before outlining or drafting; then follow the existing holds, source reading and calendar selection.
- **Discovery and comments:** use the foundation to express the contribution, while answering the actual source argument. Do not project the author’s worldview onto another writer or pretend partial reading is complete.
- **Writer review and enqueue:** review each exact channel payload under the current contract. The existing runner checks contract ID, version, contract hash, payload hash and declared review coverage. Those checks bind a review; they cannot prove a model read a skill or certify philosophical quality.
- **Approved delivery:** run the existing identity, approval, duplicate, source and receipt checks. If a new foundation finding warrants changing approved prose, prepare a separate revision; do not change the approved payload during delivery or silently inherit its approval.
- **Monthly learning and Muse reports:** distinguish observed responses from interpretation. Suggest source additions, worldview events or vocabulary changes for author review. Engagement, repetition and agreement among Muses cannot adopt a belief or change the canonical writer automatically.
