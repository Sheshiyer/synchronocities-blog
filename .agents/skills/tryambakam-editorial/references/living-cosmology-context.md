# Personal context for a living cosmology

Reviewed 2026-10-01, at the author's request. This reference supports the [Noesis Writer method](noesis-writer-meaning.md) within `tryambakam-editorial`. It is an editorial synthesis of selected sources, not a complete biography, an adopted cosmological doctrine or a review of live engines.

## Governing authorial direction

The author wants to create **their own living system of cosmology through lived experience**. Meaning develops through exposure, differentiation, integration and return. The two confirmed exposure axes are scale—cosmic/abstract to bodily/lived—and encounter—familiar to unfamiliar/opposing. Their independence matters: a familiar bodily response can meet an unfamiliar interpretation; an abstract system can also be inherited and habitual.

Keep this current direction above historical profiles or generated interpretations. The author can develop new correspondences, axioms and operators without requiring an established school to ratify them. Preserve the origins of inherited terms so the new relation can be recognised as the author's construction.

The temporal framework treats context as changeable. The dynamic-update specification calls for reinterpretation when life circumstances or user feedback change. Relationship mapping includes harmonic resonance, complementarity and creative tension; its practical-outcome feedback is especially useful here. The application framework also recognises the risk of rigidity and preserves spontaneity.

These materials support a cosmology that can change in relation to life. They contain schemas, proposed procedures and historical symbolic interpretations. Their self-described verification labels, numeric confidence scores and planned update mechanisms do not establish a currently running or observed system.

The older validation matrix attempts to resolve disagreement using fixed system weights and convergence. The current authorial direction evolves that approach: convergence may reveal a relation, and disagreement may reveal an important distinction. A correct calculation, a convincing symbolic reading and an observed consequence contribute different things. The writer's mean expresses a situated centre of meaning; the older percentages do not calculate its truth.

Reading these files as context does not make old type-based instructions, timing examples or personal profiles current commands. Use them as lenses the author may question, combine, revise or outgrow. The `_System/entrodromia` repository is context for the essay seed of the same name, not proof that all of its systems define that seed.

## How this changes the writer

1. Begin with an encounter or an open lived question. Recover the author's own expression before supplying a system's explanation.
2. Keep the two exposure axes separate. Let systems reveal differences as well as connections.
3. Construct new meaning with an explicit contribution: a distinction, relation, axiom, operator or question.
4. Return the contribution to a chosen action and, when available, its actual consequences. A symbolic reading may shape action; an unexpected consequence may change the reading.
5. Keep earlier interpretations accessible. Later revision can change the meaning without changing what the historical note reported.
6. Let the cosmology remain open between articles. A field note or later encounter can reopen a published proposition. Existing cadence remains unchanged; revisits are recorded as possibilities, not automatically scheduled.

The working loop is **encounter → felt signal → expression → provisional meaning → chosen action → observed consequence → revised cosmology → next encounter**. Differentiation and integration operate throughout it. The mathematical form can become more precise as the author finds useful coordinates; it need not precede the experience.
