# Blind claim coverage — The Body Is the First Country

## Method

A fresh review agent received only the reader-facing article. It was explicitly denied the source lattice, prior ledger, skill files, and transmutation trace. The agent extracted **268 raw clause-level claim spans** before any comparison with the replacement Albedo ledger.

The raw extraction intentionally over-split the prose. It included individual predicates, definitions, rhetorical imperatives, headings, and fragments belonging to the same material assertion. Coverage certification therefore used two layers:

1. preserve the 268-span blind extraction as the discovery surface;
2. group related spans into atomic **material claims** suitable for evidence and provenance review.

The resulting ledger contains **45 material rows**. Every material span maps to at least one row; no material omission remains.

## Non-material exclusions and grouping rules

- YAML keys, link syntax, image alt text, and section headings were structural metadata, not independent evidentiary claims.
- Authorial scope markers and invitations such as “My interest...” and “Ask what crosses it” were not treated as external factual claims.
- Subject, predicate, and qualifier fragments extracted from one sentence were rolled into the same ledger row when they shared one warrant.
- Ordinary physician and geographer examples illustrate why distinctions are useful; they are not load-bearing medical or geographic claims.
- Repetition of a previously mapped thesis in the closing was mapped back to its source row unless the closing performed a new synthesis.
- The brand signature was retained as C045 because it functions as a public house-model claim even though it is not part of the essay’s argument.

## Corrections caused by the blind read

The blind extraction did more than confirm coverage. It exposed claims whose original verbs carried excess burden. The article now uses scoped language for partition effects (`can`), names the body–mind relation a proposition rather than an observation, narrows the cross-corpus sentence to the sources actually gathered, and states that later growth never proves suffering was deserved or secretly selected.
